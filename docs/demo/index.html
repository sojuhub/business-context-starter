Before you begin

Fair questions.
Plain answers.

You’re introducing AI to your business, not handing it the keys to everything.

I’ve never used AI. Is this for me?

Yes — the hands-on setup is designed around business knowledge you already have, not technical terminology. You bring real examples and review what we understood. The first pilot target is Codex on a Mac; preparing that environment is part of the setup scope. This preview itself does not install an AI tool.

Is this another AI course or chatbot?

No. It’s a business-onboarding approach: organize the information an AI assistant needs, then connect it to the workspace you use. A course can teach the tools; this setup prepares your specific business to use them. It isn’t a claim that your business will run by itself.

Why not just paste my website into a chat?

That’s a useful start. Your website may not contain current exceptions, booking rules or the way you handle a difficult customer. The intended setup brings together the material you choose, keeps a record of sources, and makes an approved brief available to later work.

Does connecting an account give AI control?

Access must be scoped and approved during a real setup. The onboarding is for reading and organizing selected business material; sending messages, publishing posts or changing bookings are separate actions. This website has no access to your email, files, calendar, social or sales accounts.

What do I keep? Can I leave?

The intended handoff includes readable business notes, instructions and a map of their sources. Private business data should stay separate from the public code. Actual storage, account access and model processing depend on the tools selected; open source does not mean all processing is local or that third-party services are free.

Is the example a live AI system?

No. Both examples were written to explain the idea. Ordinary JavaScript changes the sample reply when you edit two fictional rules. Those settings can persist in this browser; this is not proof of a new Codex session. Real AI behaviour, account connections and business results need separate testing.
